# Lifestyle Improvement Features Proposal

This document outlines feature ideas, scientific rationale, and implementation strategies for transforming the app into an advanced lifestyle and habit-building assistant.

---

## 1. Sequential "Habit Stacking" Routines
*   **The Concept:** Instead of presenting habits as isolated, random items, group them into sequenced blocks (e.g., *Morning Routine* or *Pre-sleep Wind Down*).
*   **Why it works:** Chaining a new habit to an established one (e.g., "Right after I pour a glass of water, I will write down my daily focus") dramatically increases compliance.
*   **Implementation:** Leverage the checklist structure of `SubTask` inside a single routine `Activity`. Introduce a linear UI card on the dashboard that guides the user through Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3.

---

## 2. Lifestyle & Mood Correlation Engine (Weekly Insights)
*   **The Concept:** Cross-reference logged data to show the user how their habits affect their mood and focus.
*   **Example insights generated by the app:**
    *   *"You log 25% higher mood scores (😊/🚀) on days you complete your Focus 25m activity."*
    *   *"Your highest expense category is Dining Out on days when you skip your morning routine."*
*   **Implementation:** Run a lightweight correlation algorithm inside `DashboardController` matching dates of positive `NoteEntity` moods against completed `CheckIn` logs.

---

## 3. Financial Mindfulness: The "Wishlist Cooldown"
*   **The Concept:** Combat impulse spending by locking non-essential purchases behind a timer.
*   **How it works:** Instead of adding a spent transaction directly, the user logs a "Wishlist Purchase" over a certain amount. The app puts it in a 48-hour "cooldown period." After the timer expires, the app asks: *"Do you still want to buy this?"*
*   **Why it works:** Delays dopamine-driven buying impulses and keeps users aligned with their limits in `Budget`.

---

## 4. Hydration & Focus Adaptive Reminders
*   **The Concept:** Notifications that react dynamically to the user's logs rather than fixed clocks.
*   **Examples:**
    *   *Hydration:* If it is 2:00 PM and the user has logged 0ml of water, send: *"You haven't logged water today. Have a quick glass now to stay sharp! 💧"*
    *   *Screen break:* After a 25-minute Pomodoro focus session finishes: *"Time to stand up and stretch your eyes for 2 minutes! 🧘"*
*   **Implementation:** Integrate state checks inside the local notification listener inside your background systems.

---

## 5. Evening Reflection & Wind-down Flow
*   **The Concept:** Help the user disconnect from work and devices before bed.
*   **Features:**
    *   At a set evening hour, the app enters "Wind-down Mode" (srgb/darker theme shifts).
    *   It prompts a single gratitude journal entry: *"What is one thing that went well today?"*
    *   Checklist for prep: *Log final water intake, prepare clothes for tomorrow, turn off screens.*
